# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of Goblin Gambling Co., a joke casino for WoW: Forever. Everything runs on **Goblin Gold**, a fake currency. The addon never touches your real gold, items or mail, and never posts in chat.

- **Slots:** three reels with Hearthstone wilds and a Thunderfury jackpot, bets from 10 to 1,000, autospin, a paytable, and three reel speeds (Riding Turtle, Kodo Trot, Rocket Boots).
- **Texas Hold'em vs. bots:** Copper, Silver and Gold tables against 3 to 5 regulars with their own personalities: Thorgrim, Shadowstep, Moo-Tan, Zapzap and Grubnik.
- **Hold'em with friends:** host or join a table with your party, raid or guild. Hole cards stay private until showdown, and everyone plays with their own Goblin Gold.
- **Grubnik's Loans:** broke? Take 1,000 Goblin Gold and owe 1,250. Pay it back from the Stats tab or with `/gg repay`.
- **Stats and titles:** lifetime numbers, streaks, a With friends view, and titles like Lucky Goblin and Debtor of Ratchet.
- Resizable window, minimap button, and `/gg` (or `/ggc`, `/goblin`) to open the casino.
